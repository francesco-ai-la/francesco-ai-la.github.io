On my title

I work for Accenture, in the AI & Data Strategy group, out of their Los Angeles office. I have always been client-facing, alternating "consulting" and building in the last few projects.

Building

I really enjoy these projects and the pressure from timelines. I help bring AI solutions into production. Although my formal title on recent projects has been “Quality Engineering Lead,” I typically operate in a broader capacity, owning the client relationship, leading deployment readiness, and making the technical go/no-go recommendation on whether an AI agent is ready.

"Consulting"

I enjoy these projects a bit less, since there is a lot of frameworks that I don't always understand. While I understand the value of the work we are doing for our clients, I think I have a more pragmatic approach to work to be a great consultant. However, I sincerely enjoy "strategy", coming up with a plan to work around the maze of corporate america bureaucracy and bring to life meaningful initiatives.
